# Ultimate Cataclysm sprites

Sprites from the Ultimate Cataclysm tileset for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, by its volunteer contributors.

- Source: `I-am-Erk/CDDA-Tilesets`, `gfx/UltimateCataclysm`
- Pinned revision: `aebb9c2768537e78d0471a65241f0347f91ba395`
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Modifications: composed into sprite sheets with CDDA's `compose.py`, cut back into single sprites,
  sorted by CDDA ID into categories and repacked as `.dmi` files, one per category per sprite size.
  Pixels are unchanged. Icon state names are the CDDA IDs.
