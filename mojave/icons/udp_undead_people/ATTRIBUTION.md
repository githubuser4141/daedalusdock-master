# UndeadPeople tileset sprites

Car parts from MSX++UnDeadPeopleEdition_v2, the UndeadPeople tileset for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, by its contributors.

- Modifications, in `udp_sedan.dmi`: each part turned to face all four directions. The bodywork has the black
  round its rounded edges made transparent and its glass made translucent. Wall, door and tyre sprites are
  cut from the bodywork, windshield and wheel sprites. State names are this codebase's own.
