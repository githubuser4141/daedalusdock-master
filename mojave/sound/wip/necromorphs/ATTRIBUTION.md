# DS13 necromorph audio

Source: [DS13-1.0](https://github.com/DS-13-Dev-Team/DS13-1.0), revision
`1751066f68b8dd75c778a3ae19a52915a8034ae4`.

All `.ogg` files here are unmodified copies of
`sound/effects/creatures/necromorph/<creature>/<filename>` at that revision.
The filename prefix gives the creature directory: slasher, lurker, brute,
infector, tripod, or exploder. These are from **1.0**, separately from the
existing DS13-2.0 sprite import.
